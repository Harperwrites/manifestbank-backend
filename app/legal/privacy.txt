ManifestBank™ Privacy Policy
Effective Date: April 4, 2026

1. Overview
ManifestBank™ values transparency and respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Platform, including The Ether™.

2. Information We Collect
Depending on usage, we may collect:
• Account information (email, username)
• User-submitted content (intentions, reflections, entries, messages)
• Usage data (feature interactions, timestamps)
• Device and technical information (IP address, browser type, operating system)

3. How Information Is Used
Information is used to:
• Operate and maintain the Platform
• Enable core features and personalization
• Improve functionality and performance
• Maintain platform safety and integrity
• Comply with legal obligations
ManifestBank™ does not sell personal data.

4. Data Storage & Security
Data is stored using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing
Information may be shared:
• With service providers supporting Platform operations
• When required by law or legal process
• To protect the rights, safety, or integrity of the Platform and its users

6. Artificial Intelligence Processing (Fortune – Teller)
ManifestBank™ utilizes artificial intelligence systems to provide certain features, including the Fortune Teller experience.
When you interact with AI Features:
• Your inputs may be transmitted to third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI)
• Responses are generated based on algorithmic processing of your inputs
• These interactions may be logged and processed to improve system performance, safety, and user experience

7. AI Data Sharing & Development Use
If enabled by ManifestBank™:
• Certain user interactions may be designated as “Development Content”
• Development Content may be used by AI providers to:
• Improve models
• Train systems
• Conduct research and evaluation
Important:
• Development Content may not be subject to the same data protection standards as other Platform data
• Such processing may occur under the AI provider’s role as an independent data controller
Users are advised:
👉 Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information you do not wish to be used for development purposes

8. User Notice & Consent for AI Processing
By using AI Features, you acknowledge that:
• You have been informed that your interactions may be processed by third-party AI systems
• You consent to such processing for functionality and, where applicable, development purposes
• You understand the limitations and risks associated with AI-generated outputs
Where required, ManifestBank™ may request explicit acknowledgment or consent prior to use of AI Features.

9. Restrictions on Sensitive Data
Users must not submit:
• Medical or health-related data subject to HIPAA
• Financial account credentials or highly sensitive financial data
• Personal data of children under 13
• Any information considered confidential, proprietary, or highly sensitive
ManifestBank™ is not responsible for user-submitted sensitive data entered in violation of this policy.

10. Third-Party AI Providers
ManifestBank™ may share data with third-party providers that support AI functionality.
These providers may:
• Process data on behalf of ManifestBank™
• Or process certain designated data independently for development purposes
Users acknowledge that such providers operate under their own privacy and data handling frameworks.

11. The Ether™ – Messaging & Community Data (Hybrid Model)
11.1 Hybrid Retention Approach
The Ether™ uses a hybrid data retention model:
• Some messages or interactions are retained temporarily
• Some content may be retained longer to support continuity, moderation, safety, or user experience
• Retention duration may vary by feature and system requirements
11.2 Purpose of Processing
Ether™ data may be processed to:
• Deliver communication and community features
• Enforce Platform rules and guidelines
• Prevent abuse or misuse
• Improve Platform functionality
11.3 Moderation & Review
Content may be reviewed by automated systems or authorized personnel to maintain safety, compliance, and platform integrity.
11.4 Visibility
Content shared in public or community-visible areas of The Ether™ may be visible to other users based on Platform settings.

12. Data Retention
Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill Platform purposes, legal obligations, or safety requirements.

13. User Rights
Depending on jurisdiction, users may have rights to:
• Access personal data
• Request correction or deletion
• Withdraw consent
Requests can be submitted through official support channels.

14. Children’s Privacy
ManifestBank™ and The Ether™ are not intended for individuals under the age of 18 (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). The Platform does not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If such information is discovered, it will be deleted in accordance with applicable law.

15. Policy Updates
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of any changes.

16. Contact
For privacy-related inquiries or requests, contact ManifestBank™ through official communication channels.
